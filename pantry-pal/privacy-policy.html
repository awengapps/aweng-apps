Pantry Pal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

In short: Pantry Pal keeps your pantry list on your own phone. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics. The only information that leaves your phone is a product barcode number, and only when you choose to scan one.

This policy explains what the Pantry Pal mobile app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by AWENG Apps ("we", "us").

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only:

  • The food items you add: name, quantity and expiry date.
  • Your history of items marked Used or Tossed, with dates.
  • Your reminder settings (on or off, time, and how many days before expiry).

We do not have a server for this data and we cannot see it. It is not uploaded, sold or shared. It stays on your phone until you delete it.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission only so you can scan product barcodes. The camera feed is used on the spot to read the barcode number. Photos and video are never saved, and they are never sent anywhere. You can refuse the permission and type item names instead.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode, the app sends that barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product's name and size. Only the barcode number is sent. Like any internet request, this lets Open Food Facts see your device's IP address. Their handling of that information is covered by their privacy policy. We do not receive or store the request or the result on any server of ours.

Notifications

The app asks for notification permission to remind you before food expires. These reminders are scheduled and shown by your phone. They do not use a server, and no notification data is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app (Reminders) or in your phone's settings.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account details.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos or files.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics tools.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Remove an item by tapping Used or Tossed, and use Undo within a few seconds if you tapped by mistake.
  • Delete your history with Clear history on the History screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Camera and notification permissions can be changed at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

The app is a general-purpose household tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
